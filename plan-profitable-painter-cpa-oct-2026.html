01

What we found

From the outside, the funnel is strong: a free book, a recorded book launch training and a free Profit Checkup call, all aimed at a very specific buyer. The opportunity is in how ad spend is measured. Cost per lead only matters if it is cost per lead that books a call.

Three front doors, each producing a different kind of lead

High impact

The book offer, the training replay and the Profit Checkup page each attract a different level of intent. When Meta is told to optimize for the cheapest form fill, it finds book requesters, and reported cost per lead looks fine while booked calls stay flat. Each offer needs its own campaign and its own target.

Entry offerLead intentWhat ads should optimize toward
Free book, cover shippingLow to mediumBook purchase, then checkup booked within 30 days
Book launch training replayMediumReplay watched, then checkup booked
Free Profit Checkup callHighAppointment booked and showed

Two domains, one tracking story

High impact

The main site runs on main.profitablepaintercpa.com while the funnels run on HighLevel at profitablepaintercpa.com. If pixel events, Conversions API and Google Ads conversions are not shared and de-duplicated across both, the ad platforms only see part of the journey and bid toward the wrong people.

A small, specific audience on Meta

Medium impact

US painting business owners are a narrow pool. On Meta, frequency climbs fast and the same ads stop working. Meanwhile some owners are already searching for exactly this service, and Google Search can catch that demand at the moment it exists.

What usually happens

  • Same 3 to 4 ads run for months
  • Broad interest targeting reaches homeowners, not contractors
  • Search is skipped because volume looks small

What works

  • Fresh creative every 2 weeks, cut from client testimonial videos and podcast clips
  • Audiences built from the client list and engaged viewers
  • Search on terms like "bookkeeping for painters" and "CPA for painting contractors"

Week 1Week 2Week 3Week 4
1

Audit both ad accounts and map every ad set to its funnel

Pull spend, leads and booked calls by offer for the last 90 days to set a true cost per booked checkup baseline.

Why: you cannot lower cost per lead until you know which leads turn into calls.

2

Connect HighLevel events to Meta and Google

Send Lead, Purchase, Appointment Booked and Showed from HighLevel through the Conversions API and as Google Ads offline conversions, de-duplicated across both domains.

Why: the platforms bid toward whatever signal they receive. Give them the booked call.

3

Rebuild Meta by offer with fresh creative

One campaign per offer, its own budget and its own target. New ads cut from existing painter testimonial videos and podcast clips, refreshed every two weeks.

Why: a small audience needs new creative more often, and proof from other painters converts best.

4

Launch tight Google Search campaigns

Exact and phrase match on high intent terms such as bookkeeping for painters, CPA for painting contractors and painting business taxes, with strict negatives and a cost per booked call ceiling.

Why: these searchers are already looking for the service, so they book at a higher rate.

5

Weekly report and budget moves

Cost per lead, cost per booked call and show rate by offer and channel, with budget moved toward whatever books calls cheapest.

Why: consistent decisions from one clear number, week after week.
